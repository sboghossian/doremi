The vision

An extension of your
artistic soul.

Every gesture you make comes alive as a melody, a beat, or a harmony. For anyone who feels music in their bones — who believes that creativity isn't confined to an instrument or a score.

Motion is the instrument

21 finger joints per hand, tracked in real time. Pinch, point, wave — every motion becomes a note, a beat, a harmony.

Trained on you

Record yourself moving through your favorite songs. A local model learns your style. Your gestures, your sound, your library.

Any instrument, all at once

Two hands, two instruments simultaneously. Hundreds of soundfonts built in. MIDI out to your DAW. The biggest playable library you've ever held — invisibly.

A library of skills

Publish your gestures. Download someone else's. An open marketplace where every musician adds a brick.

How it works

Three steps from
silence to song.

01

Record

Drop a Spotify link, hit record, move freely. Doremi captures your hands, the audio, and the song's structure — all at once.

02

Learn

A local model clusters your gestures and proposes mappings. Bind a pinch to a snare. A flick to a chord. Your vocabulary emerges.

03

Perform

Hands up. The world goes quiet. Move — and the room fills with sound only you could have made.
